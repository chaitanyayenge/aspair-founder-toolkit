# Contributing

Describe a real problem with the toolkit, the steps to reproduce it and the expected result. Keep examples fictional and free of personal or private member data. Test changes with `npm test` and check the browser layout at a phone width and a desktop width.

The toolkit is intentionally small and works without an AI provider or a remote service. Please preserve keyboard access, plain-language labels, editable drafts, explicit copy/download actions and the absence of input storage. Do not add traffic manipulation, rating automation, hidden links or promotional keyword blocks.

Ownership, legal agreements and business decisions require more than a generated note. This toolkit should help people ask clearer questions, rather than tell them who they should trust or what equity allocation they should sign.
